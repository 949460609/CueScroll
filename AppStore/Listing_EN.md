## Description

CueScroll helps creators speak to camera without losing their place.

Write a script once, then let clean auto-scroll keep your eyes near the lens for Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, client videos, and presentations.

WHY CUESCROLL
• Fast script library — create, edit, and reuse talking points
• Smooth scroll with adjustable speed and text size
• Mirror mode for physical teleprompter glass
• Countdown before recording so you start on beat
• On-device storage — no account, no tracking SDKs
• Free to try; Pro unlocks unlimited scripts and themes
