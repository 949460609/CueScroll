# App Review Notes (paste into App Store Connect)

## What the app does
CueScroll is a teleprompter for people who speak on camera (Reels, TikTok, YouTube, client videos). Users write a script, start playback, and read scrolling text at an adjustable speed. Mirror mode supports physical teleprompter glass.

## Demo account
No login required. All scripts are stored on-device.

## How to test
1. Launch the app and tap “Start scrolling” on onboarding.
2. Open the sample script “Welcome to CueScroll” (or create a new one with +).
3. Tap the script to enter the teleprompter. Wait for the 3-second countdown, then watch auto-scroll.
4. Tap the screen to show controls: adjust Speed / Size, toggle mirror (top-right), pause/resume.
5. Free plan allows 1 script. Creating a second script shows the Pro paywall (StoreKit).

## Privacy
No analytics SDKs. No account. No tracking. Scripts never leave the device. Privacy Policy URL is provided in App Store Connect metadata and in Settings.
