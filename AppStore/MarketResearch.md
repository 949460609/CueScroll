# Overseas App Market Research (Nov 2026)

## Category odds (iOS)

- Games: ~15% of apps clear est. $1k/mo (highest odds, hard to build)
- Weather / Graphics / Photo & Video / Finance: better than average
- Business / Education / Utilities (generic): worst odds despite huge app counts
- Free-with-no-IAP: majority of store, weakest earners → **must ship IAP/subscription**

## Niches with proven $1k+/mo density

| Niche | Approx. share making $1k+/mo | Solo-dev fit |
|---|---|---|
| Flight tracker | ~20% | Low (API cost) |
| Video editor | ~19% | Low (complexity) |
| Sobriety | ~18% | High |
| Solitaire | ~15% | Medium (game craft) |
| Language learning | ~14% | Medium |
| Fasting / sleep / workouts | strong | High but crowded |
| Teleprompter | solid utility revenue | **High** |

## Why CueScroll (teleprompter)

1. **Demand is real**: creators need scrolling scripts for Reels/TikTok/YouTube; category leaders earn in the $10K–$100K+/mo band.
2. **Indie path exists**: Teleprompter Pro ran for years as a small paid utility; newer apps still monetize via subscription + ads.
3. **MVP is shippable**: no cloud, no ML API cost, StoreKit monetization.
4. **Differentiation**: privacy / on-device / no account — common complaint against bloated competitors.
5. **Avoided fasting**: search demand declining (~−64% YoY in one US measurement) and dominated by Zero/Fastic.

US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany — English listing first; add DE/ES later for ASO gaps.

Free core (1 script) → Pro ($4.99/mo, $29.99/yr, lifetime). Matches creator willingness-to-pay for a production tool.
